## Some things you should know about me

1. I like making lists. Expect plenty of lists.
2. I'm always learning something new.
3. I repair and repurpose laptops and old tech when its fairly straightforward. (E.g. [Ripping out a laptop screen and hooking it up to a driver board to use as a second monitor](https://youtu.be/WCWD4wzmpDU))
4. I'll hack into, or reverse engineer any system that I find convenient or straightforward with some level of accessible tooling. (Thank you OSS)
5. I'd much rather adapt an existing solution than create one from scratch. A system integrator at best, a scriptkiddie at worst. 
- I'm sure building something from scratch has its merits, but I really don't want to spend time recreating the wheel most of the time. 
- I like to think that I am humble enough to admit that I can't do everything myself.
